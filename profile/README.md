<p align="center">
  <b><a href="https://fshy.ajam.dev">fshy</a></b> is a searchable catalogue of free and cheap cloud capacity, with evidence.<br>
  Every record states its quota, what a signup costs, and the date of the last check.
</p>

<br>

### &nbsp;What you'll find

- **[Storage](https://fshy.ajam.dev/t/storage/)** · **[Databases & KV](https://fshy.ajam.dev/t/databases-kv/)** — object storage, drives, hosted databases
- **[Compute](https://fshy.ajam.dev/t/compute/)** · **[Inference](https://fshy.ajam.dev/t/inference/)** — machines, sandboxes, GPU time, model APIs
- **[Egress & tunnels](https://fshy.ajam.dev/t/egress/)** · **[IP & proxies](https://fshy.ajam.dev/t/ip-proxy/)** — ways to get traffic out or in
- **[Email](https://fshy.ajam.dev/t/email/)** · **[Domains & DNS](https://fshy.ajam.dev/t/domains-dns/)** · **[CI runners](https://fshy.ajam.dev/t/ci-cd-runners/)** — the plumbing around it all
- **[Debrid](https://fshy.ajam.dev/t/debrid/)** · **[Crawling](https://fshy.ajam.dev/t/crawler-scraper/)** — retrieval, search and reader APIs

<br>

### &nbsp;How to read a record

| Verdict | Meaning |
|:--|:--|
| ★ **usable** | works as stated; the read path was probed |
| **caveat** | usable, with a named limit |
| **trial** | a time-boxed or one-time offer, not a recurring allowance |
| **paid** | no free allowance; listed for its price |
| **fails** | the blocker is named: identity, egress or quota |
| **dead** | the offer or the service no longer exists |
